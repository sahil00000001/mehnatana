  • {% translate "Paid Leave Days" %} — {% translate "leave with pay." %} {% translate "Weekly Off + Holidays" %} — {% translate "paid weekly offs and holidays. The weekly offs of the month are already filled in; add paid holidays." %}

{% translate "Can I make the salary without the Excel sheet?" %}
{% url 'portal:minimum_wages' as wages_url %}{% url 'companies:payroll' as rules_url %}
{% blocktranslate %}Yes. Open the month and choose one of the two buttons. Calculate by days: write the days each person was present; the monthly salary in each person's profile is paid for those days. Calculate by take-home: write only the amount the owner pays in hand. With the Minimum-wage split on (Settings → Payroll rules) and the minimum wages of your state entered once, Basic is the minimum wage for the paid days and the rest is incentive. By take-home, PayCore calculates paid days from full-month Basic for the person's skill, fixed ESI of ₹158 for ESI-covered people and PF on Basic. The incentive makes the take-home exactly the amount after deductions. Those paid days are calculated from the amount, not an attendance record, and may be fewer even when the person worked the full month. On both pages the figures change as you type; nothing is saved until you press Save.{% endblocktranslate %}
{% translate "How do I leave a person out of the attendance sheet for now, without relieving them?" %}
{% translate "Open the person's page and press Make inactive, choosing the month from which they are left out. They are then left out of the attendance sheet, registers and salary from that month on. Press Make active again to bring them back. Finalised months never change." %}

{% translate "What is LOP?" %}
{% translate "Loss of pay: days not worked and not paid. Salary is cut by monthly salary ÷ days in the month for each LOP day, or ÷ the working days of that month if Settings → Payroll rules says so." %}
{% translate "Why is PF only on ₹25,000?" %}
{% translate "From October 2026 the PF wage ceiling is ₹25,000 (it was ₹15,000). PF is worked out on wages up to the ceiling, unless you choose otherwise in Payroll rules." %}
{% translate "Why are PF and ESI on half of the salary for some people?" %}
{% translate "Under the Labour Codes, wages for PF and ESI cannot be less than half of the total pay. If HRA and other allowances are more than half, the extra counts as wages. Raising Basic avoids this." %}
{% translate "Why does ESI continue after a raise?" %}
{% translate "ESI coverage is fixed for six months at a time (April–September, October–March). A person covered at the start of the period stays covered until it ends, even after a raise above ₹21,000." %}
{% translate "Old or new tax regime?" %}
{% translate "The new regime is the default and has no tax up to ₹12 lakh taxable income. The old regime suits people with large deductions (80C, home loan, HRA). Ask the employee; your CA can help. Set it on the employee page." %}
{% translate "I made a mistake after finalising" %}
{% translate "Only the latest finalised month can be reopened. Open it, press Reopen this month and write the reason. Correct the sheet, upload again and finalise. Payslips then say 'Revised'." %}
{% translate "Can I cut money for damage or loss?" %}
{% translate "Only as the Code on Wages allows: for damage or loss caused by the employee's neglect, after giving them a chance to explain. Total deductions should not be more than half of the salary." %}
{% translate "What must an appointment letter contain?" %}
{% translate "The OSH Code makes an appointment letter compulsory for every employee, with particulars such as name, designation, skill category, wages, social security and the LIN of your establishment. Our appointment letter always prints this table." %}
{% translate "When must salary be paid?" %}
{% translate "Monthly salary before the 7th of the next month. When someone leaves, final dues within two working days." %}
