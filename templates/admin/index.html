What to use here

Companies, employees, salary and reports are not here. They are on the main site: All companies. Add a company there, then press Open to work on it.

Statutory tables (PF and ESI rates, professional tax slabs, income tax slabs, due dates) are already filled in with the current government figures and are shared by every company. Change them only when the law changes, by adding a new row from the date it applies. Audit log only shows a record of every change; nothing can be edited there.
