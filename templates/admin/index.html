What to use here

To start a new business:

  1. Companies → Add. Fill the boxes in the first part (code, names, address, state, PIN code) and the first salary month. Everything else can be left as it is and changed later.
  2. In the Companies list, tick the business, choose Create owner login in the Action list and press Go. Copy the username and temporary password shown, and give them to the owner.

When an owner forgets the password or is locked out: Logins → tick the login → Reset password or Unlock.

Leave the rest alone: Statutory tables (PF and ESI rates, professional tax slabs, income tax slabs, due dates) are already filled in with the current government figures. Change them only when the law changes, by adding a new row from the date it applies. Audit log only shows a record of every change; nothing can be edited there.
